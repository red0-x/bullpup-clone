How it works

Four steps. One calm host.

Step #1

Snap a photo

Point your phone at the leak, the cold hot tub, the dead AC. Add a note like "guests arrive Friday 4pm."

Step #2

Bullpup sniffs it out

A draft of what's wrong, the trade you need and how urgent it is. You confirm or change it with one tap.

Step #3

Call the top 3

Your best three for that property, with a score and a plain reason. A friendly message is drafted, you send it.

Step #4

The bench gets smarter

Showed up? On time? Every outcome updates the scores, so next time the right person is first.

Scores you can read

Math, not vibes.

Every score is calculated from real outcomes and comes with a one-line reason. The AI never picks your contractors.
